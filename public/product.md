# Email Nexus — Your Gmail, now on WhatsApp

Find any invoice, save any attachment, and manage your inbox without ever leaving WhatsApp. Privacy-first, AI-powered.

---

## 🚀 Product Overview

**Email Nexus** is a premium, high-density SaaS platform designed to bridge the gap between your email workspace and your daily communication hub. By securely integrating Gmail with WhatsApp using official Google OAuth 2.0 and enterprise AI engines, Email Nexus enables you to perform deep searches, summarize your inbox, and manage email files securely—entirely inside WhatsApp.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🔍 Instant AI Search
* **Natural Language Queries**: Simply ask *"Hey Nexus, find my last AWS invoice"* or *"Did I get any email from Vercel yesterday?"*.
* **Deep Parsing**: The AI analyzes context, semantic meaning, and sender details to find exactly what you need without complex search filters.

### 2. 📁 Secure Attachments Vault
* **Auto-Extraction**: Instruct Nexus to automatically parse incoming emails for attachments (PDFs, receipts, boarding passes, images).
* **One-Click Saving**: Extracted documents can be pushed straight to your personal, encrypted Attachments Vault and downloaded directly inside WhatsApp.

### 3. ⚡ Dynamic AI Digests
* **Inbox Summaries**: Request a quick bulleted summary of your unread correspondence: *"Summarize my unread emails from today"*.
* **Intelligent Prioritization**: The system extracts key action items and critical updates so you only open your desktop when necessary.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. **Secure Sync**: Link your Gmail securely via Google OAuth 2.0 (we request only read-only search and file extraction metadata).
2. **Add to WhatsApp**: Click your custom onboarding invite link to add the verified Email Nexus AI contact to your phone.
3. **Manage Ephemerally**: Start querying, searching, and filing your emails on-the-go using standard chat commands.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Security First

Your trust is our highest priority:
* **Zero Persistent Storage**: We do not store your email body contents. All parsing runs in ephemeral sandbox environments.
* **No AI Training**: Your personal data is never used to train machine learning models.
* **End-to-End Encryption**: Communication between WhatsApp, our servers, and your email providers is fully encrypted using 256-Bit SSL standards.
